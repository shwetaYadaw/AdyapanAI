## 📚 What's New: Circular Seating Test

**Problem**: 8 people (P,Q,R,S,T,U,V,W) in circle facing centre

**4 Sub-Questions**:
1. Who NOT neighbours? → R and V
2. Right of V? → T
3. Position of S? → Second right of P
4. Has U,W as neighbours? → S

**Key**: Solve using 6 constraints together
